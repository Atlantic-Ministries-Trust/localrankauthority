    FAQ

    Common questions

    What's the difference between AI search visibility and AI agents?

    AI search visibility helps customers find and choose you when they ask tools like ChatGPT or Google AI Mode. AI chat and voice agents answer those customers once they contact you. One brings people in, the other makes sure nobody slips away.

    Can you guarantee my business gets recommended by ChatGPT?

    No one honestly can. AI answers change by question, location, and over time. We strengthen the signals these tools rely on and track your mentions across a consistent set of customer questions.

    What is rank and rent?

    We build a website that ranks for a service in a specific city, such as "fence installation in Wilmington." The calls it generates go to one business in that territory for a monthly fee.

    Will an AI receptionist sound robotic?

    Modern voice agents sound natural and handle common questions well. We script them around your services, test them before launch, and route anything unusual to you.

    Do I own my website?

    Yes. Your domain, content, and site are yours. Rank-and-rent lead sites are the exception: we own those and rent the leads.

    How fast will I see results?

    Website, chat, voice, and follow-up tools work from launch day. Search rankings usually take a few months to build, depending on your market's competition.
