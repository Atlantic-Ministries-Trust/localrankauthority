    FAQ

    Common questions

    Do I need every service?

    No. We start with your goal and what you already have. You may need one focused project or a few pieces working together. The recommendation should fit your business, not a package.

    Can you work with my existing website?

    Yes, that's often the best place to start. We review the platform, content, and how customers move through it before recommending improvements or a rebuild.

    What does the free audit include?

    It tests your homepage's mobile speed with Google PageSpeed, checks the SEO basics (titles, descriptions, headings, schema, sitemap, and robots.txt), looks up where you rank on Google, Bing, and DuckDuckGo for your service and city, and asks AI assistants who they recommend. You see a score for each area right away and can unlock the full findings and fixes by email. Any check that can't run is marked as not scored.

    What's the difference between AI search visibility and AI agents?

    AI search visibility helps customers find and choose you when they ask tools like ChatGPT or Google AI Mode. AI chat and voice agents answer those customers once they contact you. One brings people in, the other makes sure nobody slips away.

    Can you guarantee my business gets recommended by ChatGPT?

    No one honestly can. AI answers change by question, location, and over time. We strengthen the signals these tools rely on and track your mentions across a consistent set of customer questions.

    What is rank and rent?

    We build a website that ranks for a service in a specific city, such as "fence installation in Wilmington." The calls it generates go to one business in that territory for a monthly fee.

    Will an AI agent know everything about my business?

    Only what you approve. Each agent gets a knowledge base you sign off on, clear limits, and testing before launch. We decide together what it can answer, which details it collects, and when it hands a question to you.

    Will an AI receptionist sound robotic?

    Modern voice agents sound natural and handle common questions well. We script them around your services, test them before launch, and route anything unusual to you.

    Do I own my website?

    Yes. Your domain, content, and site are yours. Rank-and-rent lead sites are the exception: we own those and rent the leads.

    Can you connect booking, payments, and follow-up?

    Yes. We can set up forms, appointment booking, deposit links, CRM connections, and follow-up texts and emails around the tools you already use. Any platform or messaging fees are spelled out in your proposal.

    How much does a project cost?

    Scope comes first. Your goals, your market, integrations, and ongoing support all affect the price. You get a written plan with deliverables and a fixed price before you commit to anything.

    Do you only work with businesses in New York?

    We're based in New York's Capital Region and work with local businesses in other markets too. Most of the work happens online, so distance isn't a problem.

    How fast will I see results?

    Website, chat, voice, and follow-up tools work from launch day. Search rankings usually take a few months to build, depending on your market's competition.
