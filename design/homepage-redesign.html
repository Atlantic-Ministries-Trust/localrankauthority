Can you help a business like mine?

We work with businesses selling services or products, including those serving a defined area and those selling nationwide. Share your business, market, and goal so Michael can confirm whether the work is a fit. A local search project, online store, and AI follow-up system need different approaches.

Will I have to replace my website or existing tools?

Not automatically. We review what you already have and what is preventing it from doing the job you need. The recommendation may be focused improvements, a supported integration, or a rebuild where the findings justify one.

How much of my time will this take?

Before you commit, the project scope identifies what we handle and what you need to provide or approve. That can include access, accurate business information, examples, and feedback. The audit includes a 45-minute walkthrough; implementation needs depend on the project.

What do I get from the paid audit that the free score does not provide?

The free score gives you automated checks of website basics, service-and-city search rankings, and selected AI answers. The $2,000 audit adds expert review, competitor research, prioritized recommendations, ready-to-use copy, a landing page, and a walkthrough. It is a plan you can use independently.

What will it cost and am I committing to a monthly plan?

The audit is $2,000. Implementation packages and optional ongoing support are priced separately, with their scope and terms provided before you commit. View Implementation Options for starting prices. Software and messaging fees, when applicable, are identified in your proposal.

Do I own the website and content you create?

Yes, as set out in your agreement. Rank-and-rent lead sites are a separate arrangement: Local Rank Authority owns those sites and rents the leads. Any third-party software, licensing, and platform requirements are identified in the proposal.
