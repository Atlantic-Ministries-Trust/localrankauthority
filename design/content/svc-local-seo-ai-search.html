Questions

Local SEO & AI Search: common questions

What's the difference between local SEO and AI search visibility?

Local SEO gets you into Google's map results and regular search. AI search visibility gets you named when people ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, or Google's AI. They rely on many of the same signals, so we work on both together.

Is this the same as GEO or AEO?

Yes. Generative engine optimization (GEO) and answer engine optimization (AEO) are names for the AI half of this work: making your business easy for AI tools to find, understand, and recommend.

Can you guarantee a top-three ranking or an AI recommendation?

No one honestly can. Results depend on your market, your competitors, and your location. We improve the signals that matter and show you the numbers every month.

How long until I see results?

Fixes to your profile and website take effect within weeks. Steady ranking gains usually take three to six months, and less competitive markets often move faster.

Do you work with businesses outside the Capital Region?

Yes. We're based near Albany and work with local businesses across the US. The work is the same wherever your customers are.

Will I have to write anything?

No. We write the pages and answers, and you approve them. We'll ask you for details only you know, like the jobs you most want more of.
