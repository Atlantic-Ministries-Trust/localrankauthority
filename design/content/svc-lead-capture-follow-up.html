Questions

Lead Capture & Follow-Up: common questions

What is an estimate builder?

A tool on your website where customers pick the job details and see a ballpark price right away. To get an exact quote, they leave their contact details, so you get a lead with the job scope already filled in.

Will automatic texts annoy my customers?

They shouldn't. Messages go only to people who asked for a quote or booking, are written in your voice, stop when someone replies or opts out, and you approve every one.

Do I need a CRM?

No. We can send leads to your phone and email, or connect to a CRM you already use.

Will a price range scare people off?

Usually the opposite. A range answers the first question most customers have and filters out jobs that were never a fit. You decide which numbers to show.

What does it cost to run?

Some tools have monthly platform or texting fees. We list every one in your proposal before you commit.
