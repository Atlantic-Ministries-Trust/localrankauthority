Working Together

Can you help a business like mine?

We work with businesses selling services or products, including those serving a defined area and those selling nationwide. Share your business, market, and goal so Michael can confirm whether the work is a fit. A local search project, online store, and AI follow-up system need different approaches.

Will I have to replace my website or existing tools?

Not automatically. We review what you already have and what is preventing it from doing the job you need. The recommendation may be focused improvements, a supported integration, or a rebuild where the findings justify one.

How much of my time will this take?

Before you commit, the project scope identifies what we handle and what you need to provide or approve. That can include access, accurate business information, examples, and feedback. The audit includes a 45-minute walkthrough; implementation needs depend on the project.

Yes, as set out in your agreement. Rank-and-rent lead sites are a separate arrangement: Local Rank Authority owns those sites and rents the leads. Any third-party software, licensing, and platform requirements are identified in the proposal.

Search and AI Visibility

What does search visibility work include?

Depending on your business, it can include technical SEO, useful product or service content, relevant business listings, reviews, and clearer information about what you offer. For businesses serving a defined area, it can also include Google Business Profile and service-area content. Your proposal identifies the work that fits your market.

Can you guarantee a number one ranking or an AI recommendation?

No. Search rankings and AI answers depend on systems we do not control. We work on the information, accessibility, content, and credibility that support discovery, then report what changes.

What are GEO and AEO?

Generative engine optimization and answer engine optimization are terms used for work that helps answer systems find, understand, and reference information about a business. This overlaps with SEO, clear content, consistent business facts, and credible references elsewhere online.

How do you measure AI visibility?

We check a defined set of questions relevant to your business and record whether you are mentioned, which competitors appear, and the sources shown. We compare those observations over time alongside available referral traffic and inquiries. They are a sample of answers, not a complete measure of every customer’s experience.

How soon will I see results?

Changes to a form, follow-up process, or website can begin helping once they are launched and tested. Search visibility takes time and varies with your starting point, competition, and scope. Your plan identifies what can be delivered quickly and what needs longer observation.

AI and Customer Follow Up

Can I try your AI receptionist before discussing a project?

Yes. Call Miles on our main line at (518) 460-1010 and say you are trying the demo. Ask questions or try a short example for your type of business. Contact details are not required to explore. If you want Michael to follow up, ask Miles and he will switch to the normal inquiry process.

What is the difference between AI search visibility and AI automation?

AI search visibility concerns how your business appears when people use AI tools to research a product, service, or provider. AI automation helps with tasks inside your business, such as answering common questions, collecting inquiry details, routing requests, and following up.

What can an AI chat agent or receptionist do?

Depending on the approved scope and integrations, it can answer common questions, collect contact and job details, direct people to a booking process, and hand off requests that need a person. Its permissions and limits are agreed before launch.

Will the AI know my business and when to hand off?

We build its knowledge around business information you approve and test common situations before launch. We agree on what it can answer, what it should collect, and when it should send the conversation to you. Ongoing review matters because business information changes.

Can you connect my booking system or CRM?

We review the tools you use and confirm what their integrations support before promising a connection. Your proposal identifies the systems, scope, and any software or messaging fees. Some connections may need a different approach or additional development.

Ads, Landing Pages, and Jingles

How much do ads cost, and who pays Google?

You set the monthly ad budget with us before launch. Where agreed, Google bills your ad spend directly to you in an account you own. Our setup, management, and any production are separate and listed in your proposal.

How do you track whether ads are working?

We set up conversion tracking for calls, forms, and bookings where it can be set up, and report clicks separately from real inquiries. Click rate is clicks divided by impressions, so five clicks per thousand impressions is 0.5%. There is no universal good number, so we judge results by qualified inquiries and sales.

How do YouTube ads charge, and do I need a video?

Viewers can skip after five seconds. With cost-per-view bidding you are typically charged when someone watches 30 seconds (or the whole ad if it is shorter) or interacts with it. Other bidding strategies charge for impressions. If you don't have a video, we can script and produce one within the agreed scope.

What does a custom jingle include?

A creative brief, original lyrics and melody, production, and the versions you agree on for ads, videos, podcasts, and your website. You hear the direction before final production, and revisions and usage rights are written into your proposal.

Who owns the ad accounts, landing pages, and jingles?

Ad accounts are set up in your name where agreed, so you keep them and their history. Landing pages built for your business and jingle usage rights are yours as set out in your agreement.
