Questions

Google Business Profile & Listings: common questions

What is a Google Business Profile?

It's the free listing that shows your business on Google Maps and in the map results on Google Search, with your hours, reviews, photos, and phone number. For most local businesses it brings in more calls than the website does.

Why does it matter if my listings match?

Google and AI assistants compare your details across many sites. When your name, address, and phone number agree everywhere, they can trust they have the right business, and you're more likely to be shown.

I work from home. Will my address be public?

It doesn't have to be. Service-area businesses can hide their address on Google and show the towns they serve instead. We set that up correctly.

Do I need to give you my Google password?

No. You add us as a manager on your profile, and you stay the owner. You can remove our access at any time.

Can you remove bad reviews?

Only reviews that break Google's rules, such as spam or reviews from someone who was never a customer, can be reported for removal. For the rest, a calm, helpful public reply works best, and we can help you write it.
