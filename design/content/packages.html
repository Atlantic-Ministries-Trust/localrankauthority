Pricing questions

The fine print, in plain English.

Do I have to start with the audit?

Yes, for setup packages. It's how we know which problems are worth fixing first, and the full $2,000 is credited if you start a package within 30 days.

What isn't included in the price?

Costs paid to other companies, such as ad spend, call tracking, phone and texting fees, and software subscriptions. Your proposal lists each one before you commit.

Can I pick parts of a package?

Yes. Packages are a starting point. Your written proposal sets the exact scope and a fixed price.
