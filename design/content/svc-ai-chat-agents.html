Questions

AI Chat Agents: common questions

Will the chat agent know everything about my business?

Only what you approve. Each agent gets a knowledge base you sign off on, clear limits, and testing before launch.

What if a customer asks something it doesn't know?

It says so, takes their details, and passes the question to you. It's set up not to guess.

Can it quote prices?

It can share the price guidance you approve, such as typical ranges, and explain what affects the final price. Exact quotes stay with you.

Is this the same as an AI receptionist?

They're related. The chat agent answers on your website, and the AI receptionist answers phone calls. Many businesses use both.

Does it work on my current website?

Usually, yes. It's added with a small piece of code that works on most website platforms.
