What we do

Topic planning from real questions

We build your topics from the questions customers ask, the concerns that slow their decision, and the products, services, and markets you want more of.

Articles on your website

Each topic becomes a useful article published on your own site, written in your voice and checked with you for accuracy.

Matching podcast episodes

Each article gets a corresponding audio episode with its own episode page and useful written text, so listeners and readers find the same answer.

Distribution to podcast apps

Episodes go out through a podcast feed (called RSS) to the services we agree on, such as Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Amazon Music, and YouTube, where setup and publishing are supported.

Narration options, with your permission

Episodes can use a narrator or, only with your explicit permission, an approved AI version of your voice. We never present generated audio as something you recorded yourself. Options are set in your proposal.

A monthly check-in

Each month you see what was published, the evidence of reach and response we can measure, the next topics, and anything we suggest changing.

Questions

Content Marketing & Podcast Syndication: common questions

Do I have to record anything?

No. You share your knowledge in a short conversation or written notes. Episodes can be narrated for you, or use an approved AI version of your voice if you give explicit permission.

How do you choose topics?

From the questions your customers actually ask, the concerns that hold them back, and the services or products you want to sell more of. You approve the plan.

Will anything publish without my approval?

No. You approve the facts and the script for every article and episode before it goes out.

Where will my podcast appear?

On the services we agree on, such as Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Amazon Music, and YouTube, where setup and publishing are supported. Each service has its own review and rules, so being submitted isn't the same as being accepted everywhere.

What do you need from me?

Your business information, your expertise on each topic, access to publish on your website where needed, and your approval of each piece. How much time that takes depends on the schedule you choose.

Who owns the content?

Your articles and episodes are published for your business. Ownership and any licensing are written into your agreement.

Will this get me more customers?

It gives buyers useful answers from you, which can help them find and choose you over time. We report what we can measure and don't promise rankings, AI mentions, or a number of leads.
