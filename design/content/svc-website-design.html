Questions

Website Design: common questions

How long does a new website take?

Most service business sites take four to eight weeks, depending on the number of pages and features.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. Sometimes improving what you have is the better choice. We review the platform, content, and how customers move through it before recommending a rebuild.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, content, and site are yours, and everything is set up in your name.

Will I lose my Google rankings if I switch?

Not if it's done carefully. We redirect every old page to its new home and check search results after launch.

Can I make changes myself?

Yes. You can update text, photos, and hours yourself, and we're available for anything bigger.

Do you write the content?

Yes. We write it from a short interview with you, then you approve it.
