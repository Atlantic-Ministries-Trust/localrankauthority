Questions

Custom Jingles: common questions

Do jingles still work?

Sound is one of the most memorable parts of an ad. A short tune built around your name or number can help people remember you long after the ad ends.

Will it sound cheesy?

Not unless you want it to. Styles range from classic radio jingles to modern, understated sound logos, and you hear a sketch before anything is final.

Who owns the jingle?

You get the rights to use it in your own advertising, set out in writing in your agreement.

Where can I use it?

Radio, TV and streaming ads, social video, your website, trade shows, and your phone's on-hold message.

Can it include my phone number?

Yes. Building the number into the melody is one of the most effective ways to make it stick.
