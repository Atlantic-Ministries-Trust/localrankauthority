Questions

Custom Jingles & Audio Branding: common questions

Where can I use it?

In video ads, YouTube, radio and streaming ads, your podcast, your website, landing-page videos, and other uses written into your agreement.

What files do I get?

The versions and formats agreed in your proposal, ready for your ads, videos, and website.

Are revisions included?

Yes, as set out in your proposal. You hear the direction before final production so changes happen early.

Who owns it, and how are rights documented?

Your usage rights are written into your agreement before production starts.

Will it sound cheesy?

Not unless you want it to. Styles range from classic jingles to short, understated sound logos, and you approve the direction first.

Will a jingle increase sales?

We aim to make your name easier to remember. We can't promise recall, rankings, or sales from a jingle alone.
