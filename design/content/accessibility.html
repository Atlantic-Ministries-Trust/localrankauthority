Accessibility

Everyone should be able to use this site, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification, or captions.

Draft for review. Run an accessibility check on the finished site before publishing, so this page describes it accurately.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, the standard most widely used in the US. We build the sites we make for clients toward the same standard.

What we've done

  • Text and buttons with strong color contrast.
  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline.
  • Headings, labels, and landmarks that screen readers can follow.
  • Text descriptions for meaningful images, and form fields with clear labels and error messages.
  • Pages that work when zoomed to 200% and on small screens.
  • Animation that turns off when your device is set to reduce motion.

Known limits

Some parts come from other providers, such as the booking calendar and the AI chat and voice demos, and may not fully meet the standard.
