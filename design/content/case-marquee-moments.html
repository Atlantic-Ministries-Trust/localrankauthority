The business

Marquee Moments rents 4-foot light-up marquee letters, numbers, and symbols for weddings, birthdays, graduations, proposals, and corporate events across Albany, Saratoga, Troy, Schenectady, and the rest of the Capital Region. Every rental is full service: delivery, professional setup, and removal. Packages start at $499.

The challenge

  • No name to start with. The domain was registered in January 2024. Nobody was searching for “Marquee Moments”, so every customer had to come from a general search.
  • People search for it in different ways. “Marquee letters,” “light up letters,” “marquee letters for a wedding.” The business had to show up for all of them, town by town.
  • Established competitors. The other names in Google's map results carry 27 to 109 reviews. Marquee Moments started with none.
  • A premium price. At $499 and up, people need to trust you before they ask for a quote, and they want a price before they call.

What's next

A case study that only shows wins isn't much use to you. Here's what the same research says to work on next.

  • Reviews. Two Google reviews against competitors with 27 to 109 is the biggest gap. Every finished event should end with a simple, honest request for a review.
  • Troy, and weddings outside Albany. Marquee Moments doesn't show up yet for “marquee letter rental Troy NY” or for wedding searches in Saratoga, Schenectady, and Troy. Those pages need real local detail: venues served, photos from events there, and reviews from those towns.
  • More local detail on every town page. The service-area pages share a lot of the same wording. Adding what's genuinely different about each place makes them more useful to visitors and to Google.
  • Tracking bookings. Rankings are a means to an end. Next we'll report inquiries and booked events alongside rankings, so the results above can be measured in revenue.
