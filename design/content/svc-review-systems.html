Questions

Review Systems: common questions

Is it allowed to ask customers for reviews?

Yes. Google allows and encourages asking for reviews. What it doesn't allow is paying for reviews, offering discounts or gifts in exchange, or asking only customers you expect to be happy.

Can I send unhappy customers somewhere else first?

No. Screening customers before asking for a review is called review gating, and Google doesn't allow it. We ask every customer the same way.

What about negative reviews?

A prompt, calm, helpful reply shows future customers how you treat people. We draft replies for you to approve. Reviews that break Google's rules can be reported.

Will the requests come from my business?

Yes. Texts and emails come from your business name, written in your voice.

Do reviews help with AI search too?

Yes. AI assistants often draw on reviews when they recommend businesses, so a steady flow of genuine reviews helps both Google rankings and AI answers.
