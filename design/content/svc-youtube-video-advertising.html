Questions

YouTube Video Advertising: common questions

Do I pay when someone skips?

It depends on the bidding strategy. Viewers can skip after five seconds. With cost-per-view bidding, you're typically charged when someone watches 30 seconds (or the whole ad if it's shorter) or interacts with it, whichever comes first. Other bidding strategies charge for impressions. We'll explain the option we recommend for you.

Will my ad show on TV?

YouTube ads can appear on television screens through the YouTube app, and reports show device types. That's one possible placement, not the same as buying traditional TV, and we can't promise a specific share of TV viewing.

Do I need a jingle?

No. A short audio hook can help people recognize you, but your ad can open with your name and a clear message without one.

How much should I spend?

We agree on a monthly ad budget with you before launch. Ad spend is billed by Google directly where agreed, separate from our production and management.

How will I know if it worked?

We report views, clicks, and the inquiries we can track, and keep them separate. Views alone don't prove sales, so we look at what happened after.

Can you target just my town?

We can target locations and audiences that fit your business. Location targeting uses Google's signals, so it's close, not an exact boundary.
