Questions

Marketing & Business Consulting: common questions

What kind of consulting is this?

Practical consulting on marketing, getting customers, handling inquiries, and the workflows around them. It isn't legal, tax, or financial advice.

Will you just recommend your own services?

We start with your goals and what you already have. Sometimes the best answer is a small fix you can make yourself, or keeping a tool you already pay for.

How is this different from the Growth Audit?

The Comprehensive Growth Audit is a fixed $2,000 review with a detailed report, delivered in 5 business days. Consulting is a conversation-led way to set priorities and can cover more of how your business handles customers. Michael will suggest which fits.

Do I have to commit to a monthly plan?

No. Ongoing review is optional and agreed separately.

Can you work with my current team and tools?

Yes. We'll look at what you have before recommending anything new, and be clear about what any change would require.
