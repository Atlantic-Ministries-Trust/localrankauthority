What we do

Searches with buying intent

We choose keyword themes around what buyers search, such as "water heater repair near me," rather than research questions like "how to fix a water heater yourself." Broad terms aren't wrong by default; the right mix depends on your business.

Match types and negative keywords

We set how closely searches must match and add negative keywords so your budget isn't spent on searches you don't want.

Location and schedule settings

Ads show to people in the areas and at the times you serve. Location targeting is based on Google's signals, so it's close, not an exact fence.

Ads that match the search

Ad copy written for each group of searches, so the promise in the ad matches what the person typed and what they'll find.

A destination that answers the click

Each campaign points to a relevant page. If a focused landing page is needed, we scope it as part of your plan.

Tracking and reporting

Conversion tracking for calls, forms, and bookings where it can be set up, and a plain report that separates clicks from real inquiries.

Questions

Google Search Ads: common questions

Who owns the ad account?

You do. Where we agree to it, the account is in your name and you keep it and its history if we stop working together.

Who pays Google?

Your ad spend is billed by Google directly to you, where agreed. Our setup and management are separate and listed in your proposal.

How is my budget set?

We agree on a monthly ad budget with you before launch. Google spends against that budget, and we review it with you before changing it.

What do you report?

Spend, clicks, the searches that triggered your ads, and the calls, forms, or bookings we can track. We keep clicks and real inquiries separate so the picture is honest.

What happens after someone clicks?

They land on a page that matches what they searched, with a clear way to call, ask, or book. That page matters as much as the ad.

Is a high click rate good?

Click rate is clicks divided by impressions; five clicks per thousand impressions is 0.5%. A high rate on the wrong searches can still waste money, so we judge success by qualified inquiries and sales, not clicks alone.

Can you guarantee leads or lower costs?

No. We focus spending on relevant searches and show you exactly what happened, but results depend on your market, competition, and offer.
