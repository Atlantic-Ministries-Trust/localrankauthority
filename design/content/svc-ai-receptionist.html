Questions

AI Receptionist: common questions

Will an AI receptionist sound robotic?

Modern voice agents sound natural and handle common questions well. We script them around your services, test them before launch, and route anything unusual to you.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Your existing number forwards calls to the receptionist when you choose, such as after hours or when you don't pick up.

What happens with emergencies?

You decide. It can mark the call urgent and text you immediately, transfer the call, or book the first available slot.

Can callers reach a person?

Yes. It can take a message for a callback or transfer the call, based on rules you set.

Is the call recorded?

Calls can be recorded and transcribed so you have the details. Callers are told when recording is on, as the law requires.
