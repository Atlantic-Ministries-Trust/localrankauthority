SMS Terms

How we text the people who contact us, and how to stop at any time.

Draft for review. Your texting provider will review this page when it registers your number. Confirm the details match your setup before it goes live.

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Program

Local Rank Authority sends text messages to people who contact us about our marketing services, from our business number (518) 460-1010. Messages include replies to your questions, appointment confirmations and reminders, and follow-ups about the request you made. We don't send texts to lists we bought, and we don't text on behalf of other businesses from this number.

How you opt in

You agree to receive texts when you text or call our number, or when you give us your mobile number and check the box agreeing to texts on one of our forms. Agreeing to texts isn't a condition of buying anything.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with your request.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. We'll send one message confirming you've been unsubscribed, and then no more. To start again, reply START.

Help

Reply HELP for help, call (518) 460-1010, or send us a message.

AI assistance

Some replies may be written by our AI assistant using information Michael has approved. You can ask to speak with Michael at any time.

Privacy

We use your number only to communicate with you about your request. Mobile numbers and text-messaging consent are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing. See our Privacy Policy.

Carriers

Carriers aren't liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Supported carriers include major US carriers.
