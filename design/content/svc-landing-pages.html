Questions

Landing Pages & Video Landing Pages: common questions

Does it work with my existing website?

Usually, yes. The page can live on your current site or a connected page, depending on your platform. We'll check before we start.

Who owns the page?

Your landing pages are built for your business and are yours, as set out in your agreement. Any platform or software costs are listed in your proposal.

Will a video make it convert better?

Not automatically. Video helps when it answers a real question faster than text. The page has to work without it too.

Should every landing page be hidden from Google?

No. Some campaign pages are best kept out of search results and others are useful to index. We decide page by page, and we don't mass-produce near-identical town pages.

Do you offer a guarantee?

We'll only show a guarantee your business actually offers. We won't invent one to make a page look stronger.

Is this the same as the landing page in the Growth Audit?

The Growth Audit includes one finished landing page for your top service. Additional pages, campaign pages, and video pages are scoped separately.
