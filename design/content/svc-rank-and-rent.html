Questions

Rank & Rent Lead Sites: common questions

What is rank and rent?

We build a website that ranks for a service in a specific area, such as "gutter cleaning in Saratoga County." The calls it brings in go to one business in that territory, for a monthly fee.

Who owns the lead site?

We do. You rent access to its calls and inquiries. Territory, exclusivity, lead handling, and fees are agreed in writing before you start.

How many leads will I get?

It depends on the market and how well the site ranks. Lead volume is never guaranteed, and you see every lead in your monthly report.

Are the leads shared with other companies?

No. Each territory goes to one business only.

What if I want my own website instead?

That's often the better long-term choice, and we build those too. Rank and rent suits owners who want calls now without managing a site.
