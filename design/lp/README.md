## What you chose

- **Ads on:** Google Search, Microsoft Ads, Meta
- **Main offer:** the free score, with a call as backup
- **Targeting:** the Capital Region first
- **Prices:** none on service pages
- **Search engines:** pages can be indexed (not hidden)

## Decisions I made for you (change any of them)

1. **Which services got a page.** Eight: Local SEO & AI search, Website design, AI receptionist, AI chat agent, Lead follow-up, Review system, Custom jingles, and the Growth Audit.
   - **Left out:** Rank and rent (usually sold by outreach, not ads) and Ongoing care (bought after a project, not from an ad).
2. **Page addresses.** Each is a readable search term, like `/ai-receptionist-albany-ny`, because you want them to rank too.
   - The Local SEO page reuses `/local-seo-albany-ny`, replacing the current Albany page, so two pages don't compete for the same search.
   - The `/services/...` pages stay as the general versions. Each landing page targets "<service> in Albany."
3. **The Growth Audit page shows $2,000.** It's a fixed product, and the price is already public on the site.
4. **No navigation menu**, so visitors stay on the offer. The header shows only the logo, phone, and a Free score button. A Call / Free score bar sits at the bottom on phones.
5. **The form opens the homepage free score, already filled in.** It also passes along ad click IDs (gclid, msclkid, fbclid) and UTM tags, so you can see which ad produced each lead. The live homepage needs a small change to read those prefilled values. I'll make it when the pages go live.
6. **No invented proof.** Reviews and your photo are yellow placeholders. Example businesses are fictional and labeled. No statistics I couldn't verify.
7. **Ad copy is written conservatively.** No "best" or "#1," no guarantees, and review ads follow Google's rules: no incentives, and no asking only happy customers.

## What I would have asked you

1. **AI receptionist, chat agent, and jingle pages.** The free score checks websites and rankings, not missed calls or jingles, so it's a weaker match there. I'd test "Book a live demo" (or "Hear a sample jingle") as the main button on those three. Want that?
2. **Your real reviews or testimonials.** Even one per page will likely beat any copy change I can make.
3. **A sample jingle clip** for the jingles page, and recorded demo calls for the AI receptionist page.
4. **Tracking.** Paid ads need the Google Ads tag, Microsoft UET tag, and Meta Pixel. Those set cookies, so:
   - The privacy policy draft needs updating (it currently says no ad trackers).
   - You'll likely want a simple cookie notice.
   - I need your tag IDs. The conversion events are already marked on the pages.
5. **Budget and bidding.** Starting suggestion: put most of it on Google Search for the AI receptionist, Local SEO, and Website design pages, and run Meta as retargeting plus one cold test.
